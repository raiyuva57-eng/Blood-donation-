Our story

Built by people who've waited for the phone to ring.

LifeDrop started as a spreadsheet a hospital volunteer kept to track which donors were eligible each week. We turned it into something anyone can use.

What we do

LifeDrop keeps donor histories, patient blood needs, and eligibility windows in one place, so no one has to remember who gave last, or when they're due again.

Who it's for

Community blood drives, small clinics, and local donor networks who need something simple, fast, and free of paperwork — not a hospital-scale system.
